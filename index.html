Chosen for the Task

Tzedek Ambassadors on a Special Mission to Washington

A select group of ambassadors will give Washington’s decision-makers a glimpse at the warmth and beauty of Klal Yisroel’s way of life. In the process, they will open new doors for fellow Yidden in need.

To the Tzedek Ambassadors

You are Hashem’s shluchim

The Door to Washington

For more than a decade, Tzedek has been the organization Klal Yisroel turns to whenever we need a door opened in Washington. Any time something needs to be accomplished on the legislative front, this is the name and mission with the track record to get it done.

It Wasn’t Always This Way

Go back a few years and getting in front of the country’s top officials on behalf of our community wasn’t something we imagined to be possible. Klal Yisroel simply didn’t have the relationships or the necessary tools. But one heroic young man decided he wasn’t going to accept it, and it didn’t take long until the community opened our eyes to a brand new reality.

Building What Never Existed

Rabbi Moshe Margareten, Tzedek’s founder and director, poured in everything he has to build something that hasn’t existed before. Thanks to his efforts, and with tremendous Siyata Dishmaya, we can now do for our brothers what was once unthinkable. With the right advocacy, laws and policies that have sat untouched for decades are finally changing, bringing relief to thousands of Yidden in prison or in court, as well as childless couples waiting for a yeshua.

Access at the Highest Levels

R’ Moshe was B”H granted unprecedented access to the White House and the highest levels of government. But he couldn’t, and can’t, do it alone. Every day, your commitment and efforts allow Tzedek to keep knocking on legislative doors and bring about additional achievements for Klal Yisroel. This is what makes you a Tzedek ambassador.

You Are the Foundation

Our ambassadors are the foundation of everything we do. You are Hashem’s shluchim, and the ones who make sure that we can continue to be there for His children. You deserve the credit for the new laws that passed, and are the reason that so much pain and hardship has already been eased B”H.

Let’s Share the Shabbos Experience with Washington

A few months ago I got a call asking me to take part in the big Shabbat 250 put together at the President’s initiative. A few of us from Tzedek went down and spent a Shabbos in Washington.

Over the course of that weekend, we invited over a number of close aides and political staffers of Jewish background. For most of them it was the first time in their lives that they experienced a real Shabbos, with all its richness and depth. They felt a sense of the Neshama Yeseirah that Shabbos grants every Yid.

Those encounters opened doors for Tzedek in a way I could have never expected. The Jewish staffers in the White House and other D.C. offices were seeing our way of life up close for the first time. They began to better understand who we are, how we live and what we need to continue our way of life. They saw how tight-knit our community is and how hard we work to change things for our brothers and sisters, and they seemed more eager than ever to help us bring good news to our people.

And then it hit us. If one small distraction-filled Shabbos could get us this far, with so many new valuable connections, why not do it on a much bigger scale? Why not let the staffers and ambassadors really experience a beautiful Shabbos together, with heartfelt zemiros, tefilos and elevated seudos?

That’s where the Capital Shabbaton was born. The plan is to have Tzedek ambassadors come together in Washington and do some of the most meaningful and effective advocacy of all, by showing the warmth and spirit of a heimish Shabbos to the people in power. BEZ”H, we hope and expect that this will open a whole new chapter for the organization’s mission.

I’m looking forward to seeing you there as a Tzedek Ambassador. By doing this together, we will see siyata dishmaya and accomplish far more, bringing home the results we all daven for.

With Anticipation and Appreciation, Moshe Margareten Founder and President of Tzedek Association
